About Me

Data and AI leader who has built and led analytics and data engineering teams, sold and architected data platforms for enterprise customers, and now builds AI tooling that makes entire technical teams more effective.

My career has moved across the full data stack: from Tableau dashboards and BI at Charles Schwab, to leading analytics and data engineering at LiveRamp, to helping customers design modern data platforms as a Solutions Architect at Fivetran (formerly dbt Labs). Along the way I've managed onshore and offshore teams, run scrum for cross-functional groups, and partnered with executives to prioritize high-impact work.

Today I focus on where data infrastructure and AI meet. I help customers understand how an open, governed data foundation makes AI possible, and I build the internal AI systems that let a team share, version, and improve its workflows together instead of everyone maintaining their own copy.
